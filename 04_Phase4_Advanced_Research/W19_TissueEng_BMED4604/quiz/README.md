### Short Answer Solutions

**SQ1**: 
- Decellularized: Advantages — native ECM architecture, bioactive cues, already vascularized. Disadvantages — limited supply, batch variability, immune risk.
- Synthetic: Advantages — fully tunable, scalable, reproducible. Disadvantages — lacks bioactivity, requires functionalization, no native vascular network.

**SQ2**:
- Osteogenic: Runx2 (master TF), BMP-2 (GF)
- Chondrogenic: SOX9 (master TF), TGF-β1/β3 (GF)
- Adipogenic: PPARγ (master TF), insulin + IBMX (GF cocktail)

**SQ3**: O₂ diffusion limit derived from Fick's law and Michaelis-Menten consumption. Cartilage exists without blood vessels because: (1) avascular nature is physiologically normal (articular cartilage is 1-2 mm thick, O₂ can diffuse from synovial fluid); (2) low metabolic rate of chondrocytes; (3) glycolytic metabolism (anaerobic) is the primary pathway.

**SQ4**: Burst effect occurs because surface-loaded BMP-2 diffuses out rapidly. To minimize: encapsulate BMP-2 throughout the bulk (not just surface), use smaller particles, apply coating layer.

**SQ5**: Adipogenesis requires cells to be confluent/contact inhibited because: (1) cell-cell contact activates C/EBPα and PPARγ; (2) proliferating cells remain in myogenic or osteogenic fate. Osteogenesis can proceed in subconfluent cultures because BMP-Smad signaling overrides contact inhibition.

### Problem Solutions

**PQ1**:
(a) ρ_bulk = ρ_solid × (1 - ε) = 1.3 × 0.30 = 0.39 g/cm³
(b) V = π(0.5)² × 1.5 = 1.18 cm³; Mass = 0.39 × 1.18 = 0.46 g
(c) E = 2000 × (1 - 0.70)² = 2000 × 0.09 = 180 MPa
(d) Trabecular bone = 0.5-2 GPa = 500-2000 MPa. 180 MPa is 3-10× too low. Increase: increase HA content, reduce porosity, use stiffer polymer (PCL instead of PLGA).
(e) 15 mm / 0.3 mm per layer = 50 layers. Pore volume = 0.70 × 1.18 = 0.83 cm³.

**PQ2**:
(a) 0.40 + 0.55 = 0.95 (95% maximum, 5% unreleased)
(b) Day 1: 0.40×0.55 + 0.55×0.058 = 0.25; Day 7: 0.40×0.997 + 0.55×0.34 = 0.58; Day 14: 0.40×0.9999 + 0.55×0.57 = 0.71
(c) Day 1 burst = 0.25 = 25% (less than 40% due to slow initial k_slow)
(d) Day 7 effective = 10 μg × 0.58 = 5.8 μg > 5 μg (adequate)
(e) Zero-order: reservoir system (drug core + rate-limiting membrane), or osmotically-driven pump. Target: constant k₀, independent of time.
