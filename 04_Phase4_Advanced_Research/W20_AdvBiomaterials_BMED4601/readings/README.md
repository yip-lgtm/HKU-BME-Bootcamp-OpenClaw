## Learning Objectives (BMED4601)

By the end of Week 20, students should be able to:

1. Distinguish between biodegradable and permanent implants, and explain degradation mechanisms
2. Design nanoparticle drug delivery systems targeting the EPR effect (50-200 nm optimal)
3. Explain PEGylation and surface modification strategies for immune evasion
4. Compare local vs. systemic delivery approaches for specific clinical scenarios
5. Apply material property data (E values, degradation rates) to implant design
6. Understand immunomodulation strategies in advanced biomaterials
7. Evaluate 3D printing applications in biomaterial fabrication
