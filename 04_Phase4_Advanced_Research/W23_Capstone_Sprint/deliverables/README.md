## Deliverable 1: Complete Literature Review

### Requirements

**Length**: 3,000-5,000 words

**Structure**:
1. **Introduction** (300-500 words)
   - Context and significance of the problem
   - Scope of review
   - Organization of the section

2. **Background** (1000-1500 words)
   - Foundational concepts
   - Current state of technology/knowledge

3. **Current Approaches** (1000-1500 words)
   - Review of existing solutions/methods
   - Comparative analysis

4. **Gap Identification** (300-500 words)
   - What is missing?
   - Why this matters

5. **Research Questions/Hypotheses** (200-300 words)
   - Specific, testable questions
   - Clear hypotheses

### Quality Criteria

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Comprehensiveness | Covers key literature (20+ sources) |
| Critical analysis | Not just summary, but evaluation |
| Organization | Logical flow, thematic structure |
| Synthesis | Shows relationships between works |
| Originality | Your perspective and interpretation |

## Deliverable 2: Methodology Draft

### Requirements

**Length**: 1,000-1,500 words

**Sections**:
1. **Overview** (100 words)
   - Brief summary of approach

2. **Materials/Setup** (300-400 words)
   - Equipment, reagents, software
   - Sample preparation

3. **Methods** (500-700 words)
   - Step-by-step procedure
   - Justification for choices

4. **Controls & Validation** (200-300 words)
   - Positive/negative controls
   - Validation strategy

### Must Include

- Specific parameters (concentrations, temperatures, durations)
- Reproducibility details
- Quality control measures

---

## Deliverable 3: Preliminary Results

### Requirements

**Length**: 500-1,000 words + figures

**Sections**:
1. **Summary** (100 words)
   - What was done, main findings

2. **Quantitative Results** (300-500 words)
   - Statistics with error bars
   - Appropriate tests

3. **Qualitative Observations** (100-200 words)
   - Notable patterns
   - Unexpected findings

4. **Figures** (3-5 figures)
   - Clear labels and captions
   - Statistical annotations

### Statistics Checklist

- [ ] Descriptive stats (N, mean, SD)
- [ ] Appropriate statistical test
- [ ] Effect sizes reported
- [ ] Multiple comparison correction (if applicable)
- [ ] Confidence intervals

---

## Deliverable 4: Discussion Draft

### Requirements

**Length**: 500-1,000 words

**Sections**:
1. **Summary of Key Findings** (100-200 words)

2. **Interpretation** (200-300 words)
   - How do results compare to expectations?
   - What might explain findings?

3. **Limitations** (100-200 words)
   - Sample size, method constraints
   - Alternative explanations

4. **Future Directions** (100-200 words)
   - Next steps
   - Scaling up

5. **Conclusion** (50-100 words)
   - Take-home message
