# Week 24 Readings — Capstone: PG Prep (Research Proposal & Applications)

## Required Resources

### Research Proposal Writing

1. **Pepe, D.** (2019) *How to Write a Research Proposal* — Springer. ISBN: 978-3030126485
2. **Swales, J.M. & Feak, C.B.** (2012) *Academic Writing for Graduate Students*, 3rd ed. — University of Michigan Press.
3. **Creswell, J.W. & Creswell, J.D.** (2018) *Research Design*, 5th ed. — SAGE.

### PhD Application Guides

1. **Petri, H.** (2011) "How to write a PhD proposal" — *Nature Career Guide*.
2. **Van den Hoogen, J.** (2015) "Writing a research proposal" — *EMBO Reports*, 16(9), 1068-1070.

### Personal Statement Resources

1. **Stelzer, R.J.** (2018) *How to Write a Winning Personal Statement for Graduate and Professional School*, 3rd ed.
2. **Montauk, T.R.** (2015) *Graduate School Essentials: Survive the Application Process* — Peterson's.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 24, you should have completed:

1. Research proposal (3,000-5,000 words)
2. Statement of Purpose (500-1,000 words)
3. Personal Statement (500-1,000 words)
4. CV/Resume
5. Reference letter strategy
