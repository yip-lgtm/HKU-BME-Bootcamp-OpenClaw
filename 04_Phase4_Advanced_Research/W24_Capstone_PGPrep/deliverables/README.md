## Deliverable 1: Research Proposal (3,000-5,000 words)

### Structure

**1. Title** (1 line)
- Specific, informative
- Keywords for searchability

**2. Specific Aims** (~1 page, ~500 words)
```
The goal of this project is to [broad objective].

Specific Aims:
1. Aim 1: [Specific, testable aim]
2. Aim 2: [Specific, testable aim]  
3. Aim 3: [Specific, testable aim]
```

**3. Background and Significance** (~2-3 pages, ~1,000 words)
- Gap in knowledge (what we don't know)
- Impact (why it matters)
- Preliminary work (if any)

**4. Research Design and Methods** (~4-6 pages, ~2,000 words)

For each aim:
- **Approach**: How will you test this?
- **Methodology**: Specific techniques
- **Validation**: How will you know it worked?
- **Expected Results**: What will you find?

**5. Innovation** (~0.5-1 page, ~300 words)
- What's novel?
- Why haven't others solved this?

**6. Timeline** (~0.5 page, ~300 words)
```
Year 1:
  - Months 1-6: [Activity]
  - Months 7-12: [Activity]

Year 2:
  - [Continued activities]
```

**7. Expected Outcomes** (~0.5 page, ~300 words)
- Scientific contributions
- Clinical/practical impact

**8. References** (~2-3 pages, 50-100 citations)

---

### Research Proposal Quality Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Needs Work |
|----------|-----------|------|-----------|
| Clarity of aims | Clear, testable | Clear | Vague |
| Significance | Compelling impact | Important | Unclear |
| Methodology | Detailed, validated | Adequate | Missing |
| Innovation | Clearly novel | Some novelty | Derivative |
| Feasibility | Achievable in timeline | Likely | Unrealistic |
| Writing | Polished | Clear | Needs editing |

## Deliverable 2: Statement of Purpose (500-1,000 words)

### Template Structure

**Opening** (50-100 words):
*Start with a compelling statement about your interest in the field.*

**Academic Background** (100-150 words):
*Relevant coursework, skills, and academic achievements.*

**Research Experience** (250-400 words) ← MOST IMPORTANT:
*Describe 2-3 key research projects:*
- What was the project?
- What was YOUR specific contribution?
- What did you learn?
- What were the outcomes?

**Why This Program** (100-150 words):
*Specific to each program:*
- Faculty members you want to work with
- Research facilities
- Program structure

**Future Goals** (50-100 words):
*Career aspirations and how this program helps.*

### Example Opening

> "The ability to regenerate damaged tissues represents one of the most significant challenges in modern medicine. During my undergraduate research on MSC differentiation, I became fascinated by the potential of tissue engineering to transform patient care. This fascination has evolved into a passion for developing biomaterial scaffolds that can guide tissue regeneration..."

## Deliverable 3: Personal Statement (500-1,000 words)

### What Makes It Personal

NOT your academic CV. Tell YOUR story:

**Potential Topics** (choose 1-2):
- A challenge you overcame
- A defining moment
- Family or cultural influences
- Volunteer or service experience
- A unique perspective you bring

### Template Structure

**Opening** (50-100 words):
*An engaging story or moment that defines you.*

**Your Journey** (300-500 words):
*How did you get here? What shaped you?*

**Challenges and Growth** (150-200 words):
*What obstacles did you face? What did you learn?*

**Connecting to Your Goals** (100-150 words):
*How does this connect to your academic pursuits?*

**Conclusion** (50-100 words):
*Where are you going? Why does this matter?*

### Example Opening

> "I will never forget the day my grandmother received her hip replacement. Watching her walk again after months of immobility, I realized the profound impact biomedical engineering could have on quality of life. That moment planted a seed that has grown into my unwavering commitment to improving orthopedic implant technology..."

## Deliverable 5: Reference Letter Strategy

### Who to Ask

**Ideal References**:
1. **Research Supervisor** (REQUIRED) — Can speak to research abilities
2. **Course Professor** — Can speak to academic abilities
3. **Academic Mentor** — Can speak to character/growth

### What to Provide When Asking

- Your CV
- Your personal statement (draft)
- Your transcript
- List of programs and deadlines
- Talking points about yourself

### Follow-Up

- Send reminders 2 weeks before deadlines
- Thank them after submission
- Update them on results
