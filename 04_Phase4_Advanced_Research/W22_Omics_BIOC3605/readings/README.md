# Week 22 Readings — Omics (Genomics, Proteomics) (BIOC3605)

## Required Readings

### Primary Literature

1. **Lander, E.S. et al.** (2001) "Initial sequencing and analysis of the human genome" — *Nature*, 409, 860-921.
   - The landmark human genome paper from the International Human Genome Sequencing Consortium.

2. **Altshuler, D., Daly, M.J. & Lander, E.S.** (2008) "Genetic mapping in human disease" — *Science*, 322(5903), 881-888.
   - GWAS fundamentals and methodology.

3. **Aebersold, R. & Mann, M.** (2003) "Mass spectrometry-based proteomics" — *Nature*, 422, 198-207.
   - Comprehensive proteomics review.

### HKU Course Materials
- BIOC3605 — Omics and Systems Biology
  - Week 22 aligns to: Genomics, GWAS, Proteomics, Bioinformatics pipelines

### Learning Objectives (BIOC3605)

By the end of Week 22, students should be able to:

1. Understand the central dogma: DNA → RNA → Protein
2. Perform sequence alignment and BLAST-like searches
3. Interpret RNA-seq expression data (FPKM, TPM, DESeq2)
4. Apply Gene Ontology (GO) enrichment analysis
5. Understand GWAS methodology and limitations
6. Apply proteomics data interpretation
