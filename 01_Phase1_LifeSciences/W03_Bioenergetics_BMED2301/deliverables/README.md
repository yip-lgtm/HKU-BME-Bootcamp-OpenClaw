# Week 3 Deliverables — Bioenergetics, Enzymes & Metabolism

## Deliverable 1: Metabolic Calculations Problem Set

- **Type**: Problem Set
- **Deadline**: End of Week 3

### Questions

1. **ATP Yield Calculation** (25 points)
   - Calculate total ATP from oxidizing 1 molecule of glucose
   - Show all steps with equations

2. **Beta-Oxidation** (20 points)
   - Calculate ATP yield from palmitic acid (C16:0)
   - Compare with glucose on per-carbon basis

3. **Inhibitor Analysis** (25 points)
   - Explain why cyanide is rapidly fatal
   - Which ETC complex does it inhibit?

4. **Metabolic Integration** (30 points)
   - How does the cell maintain energy homeostasis?
   - Role of AMPK in metabolic regulation
