## Week 2 Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 2, students should be able to:
1. Describe the four major classes of biomolecules (carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids)
2. Explain the relationship between amino acid sequence and protein structure
3. Apply Michaelis-Menten kinetics to enzyme analysis
4. Distinguish between competitive, non-competitive, and allosteric inhibition
5. Describe the structure and function of DNA vs. RNA
6. Analyze carbohydrate structure and its relationship to biological function
