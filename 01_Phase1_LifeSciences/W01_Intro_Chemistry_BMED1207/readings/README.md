## Learning Objectives (from HKU BMED1207)
By the end of Week 1, students should be able to:
1. Identify the six bioessential elements (C, H, O, N, P, S) and explain their importance
2. Classify chemical bonds as covalent (polar/nonpolar), ionic, or hydrogen bonds
3. Apply the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation to buffer problems
4. Explain why water is essential for life (cohesion, high specific heat, solvent properties)
5. Identify functional groups in biological molecules (-OH, -COOH, -NH₂, -SH, -PO₄)
