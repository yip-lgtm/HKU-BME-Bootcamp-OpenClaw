## Deliverable 2: Lab Report — Buffer Preparation

- **Type**: Lab Report
- **Deadline**: End of Week 2 (to be submitted with Week 2 deliverables)
- **Format**: Standard scientific lab report

### Background
Buffers are essential in biological systems and laboratory settings. The blood bicarbonate buffer (CO₂/HCO₃⁻) maintains blood pH at 7.35-7.45. In this lab, you will prepare and test a phosphate buffer system similar to those found in cells.

### Objectives
1. Prepare 100 mL of 0.1 M phosphate buffer at pH 7.2
2. Verify buffer pH using pH meter
3. Test buffer capacity by adding incremental amounts of HCl
4. Compare theoretical vs. experimental results

### Materials
- Sodium phosphate dibasic (Na₂HPO₄) — FW = 141.96 g/mol
- Sodium phosphate monobasic (NaH₂PO₄) — FW = 119.98 g/mol
- 0.1 M HCl
- 0.1 M NaOH
- pH meter
- Graduated cylinders, beakers, magnetic stirrer

### Procedure (Summary)
1. Calculate required masses of Na₂HPO₄ and NaH₂PO₄ for pH 7.2
2. Dissolve salts in ~80 mL distilled water
3. Adjust volume to 100 mL
4. Calibrate pH meter with buffer standards (pH 4, 7, 10)
5. Measure actual pH of prepared buffer
6. Titrate with 0.1 M HCl in 1 mL increments
7. Record pH after each addition
8. Plot titration curve

### Calculations Required
1. **Mass calculation**:
   - Total phosphate concentration: 0.1 M
   - Ratio [HPO₄²⁻]/[H₂PO₄⁻] from Henderson-Hasselbalch
   - Individual masses for each salt

2. **Buffer capacity calculation**:
   - β = 2.303 × C_total × Ka × [H⁺] / (Ka + [H⁺])²
   - Compare theoretical vs. experimental (from titration data)

### Report Sections

1. **Title & Abstract** (100 words max)
2. **Introduction** (500 words)
   - Role of buffers in biological systems
   - Henderson-Hasselbalch equation derivation
   - Phosphate buffer significance in cells
3. **Materials & Methods** (300 words)
4. **Results** (with tables and graphs)
   - Table of titration data
   - Titration curve (pH vs. volume HCl)
   - Comparison of theoretical vs. experimental pH
5. **Discussion** (400 words)
   - Sources of error
   - Buffer capacity analysis
   - Biological relevance
6. **Conclusion** (150 words)
7. **References** (minimum 3)
