1. **Cannon, W.B.** (1932) "The Wisdom of the Body" — Classic text on homeostasis
2. **Hodgkin, A.L. & Huxley, A.F.** (1939) "Action potentials recorded from inside a nerve fibre" — *Nature*, 133, 867-868.

## Learning Objectives (from HKU BMED2302)

By the end of Week 5, students should be able to:
1. Define homeostasis and explain its importance for life
2. Describe the components of negative feedback loops
3. Explain the ionic basis of the action potential
4. Distinguish between absolute and relative refractory periods
5. Understand the physiology of major excitable tissues (neurons, muscle)
6. Connect cellular mechanisms to organ-level physiology
7. Apply homeostasis principles to clinical scenarios
