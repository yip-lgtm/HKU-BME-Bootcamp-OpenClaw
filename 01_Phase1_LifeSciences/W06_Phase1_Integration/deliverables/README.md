### Sample Questions

#### Section A: Chemistry (Week 1)
**Q1**: What is the pH of a solution with [H⁺] = 3.2 × 10⁻⁵ M?
A. 4.49
B. 4.50
C. 9.51
D. 9.50

**Answer**: B | **Explanation**: pH = -log[H⁺] = -log(3.2 × 10⁻⁵) = 4.49 ≈ 4.50

**Q2 (SA)**: Explain why water is called the "universal solvent" and how this property relates to its biological importance.

---

#### Section C: Bioenergetics (Week 3)
**Q1**: How many ATP molecules are produced from complete oxidation of ONE glucose molecule using modern P/O ratios?
A. 36
B. 38
C. 30-32
D. 42

**Answer**: C | **Explanation**: Modern estimates give 30-32 ATP per glucose (NADH = 2.5 ATP, FADH₂ = 1.5 ATP).

**Q2 (SA)**: Calculate the ΔG for ATP hydrolysis under cellular conditions where [ATP] = 10 mM, [ADP] = 1 mM, and [Pi] = 5 mM. (R = 8.314 J/mol·K, T = 310 K)

---

#### Section E: Homeostasis (Week 5)
**Q1**: In a patient with Type 2 diabetes mellitus, which of the following is the PRIMARY defect?
A. Autoimmune destruction of β cells
B. Insulin resistance with progressive β-cell failure
C. Defective GLUT4 transporters
D. Overproduction of glucagon

**Answer**: B | **Explanation**: Type 2 DM is characterized by insulin resistance combined with eventual β-cell dysfunction. This differs from Type 1 (autoimmune destruction) and Type 2 specifically involves both insulin resistance and β-cell failure.

---

### Exam Answer Key

| Section | Answers |
|---------|---------|
| A | 1. B, 2. [SA - see explanation], ... |
| B | 1. [MCQ], 2. [SA], ... |
| C | 1. C, 2. [Calculation - see below], ... |
| D | 1. B, 2. [SA], ... |
| E | 1. B, 2. [SA], ... |

**Section C Calculation Answer**:
```
ΔG = ΔG°' + RT × ln(Q)
where Q = [ADP][Pi]/[ATP]

Given:
ΔG°' = -30.5 kJ/mol
[ATP] = 10 mM = 0.01 M
[ADP] = 1 mM = 0.001 M
[Pi] = 5 mM = 0.005 M
T = 310 K
R = 8.314 J/mol·K

Q = (0.001 × 0.005) / 0.01 = 0.0005

ΔG = -30,500 + (8.314 × 310) × ln(0.0005)
ΔG = -30,500 + 2577 × (-7.60)
ΔG = -30,500 - 19,585
ΔG ≈ -50,085 J/mol ≈ -50.1 kJ/mol

Answer: approximately -50 kJ/mol
```

## Deliverable 2: Integration Essay

### Prompt
**Connect concepts from all 5 weeks to explain the following clinical case:**

Mr. Zhang, a 52-year-old man with obesity (BMI 32), presents with:
- Fasting blood glucose: 180 mg/dL (normal <100)
- HbA1c: 8.2% (normal <5.7%)
- Blood pressure: 145/92 mmHg
- LDL cholesterol: 165 mg/dL (high)
- Triglycerides: 220 mg/dL (high)

**Task**: Using concepts from Phase 1, explain:
1. The molecular basis of insulin resistance (Weeks 2-4)
2. How this leads to hyperglycemia (Week 5)
3. The connection to dyslipidemia (Weeks 3-4)
4. Why this patient is at risk for cardiovascular disease (Weeks 4-5)
5. Your integrated understanding of metabolic syndrome (All weeks)

### Essay Requirements
- **Length**: 1500-2000 words
- **Format**: Academic essay with introduction, body, and conclusion
- **Citations**: Minimum 5 references (can use textbook references)
- **Integration**: Must explicitly connect concepts across weeks

### Sample Essay Outline

```
Title: Molecular to Systemic Understanding of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Abstract (200 words)
1. Introduction (300 words)
   - Clinical presentation
   - Scope of the problem
   - Essay structure

2. Molecular Basis of Insulin Resistance (400 words)
   - Week 2: Receptor tyrosine kinase dysfunction
   - Week 3: GLUT4 trafficking defects
   - Week 4: Membrane structure effects

3. Systemic Consequences (400 words)
   - Week 5: Feedback loop disruption
   - Week 3: Altered glucose metabolism
   - Week 4: Cellular energy crisis

4. Cardiovascular Connection (300 words)
   - Week 4: Endothelial dysfunction
   - Week 3: Lipid metabolism
   - Week 5: Hypertension mechanisms

5. Conclusion (200 words)
   - Integrated understanding
   - Clinical implications
   - Future directions

References (minimum 5)
```
