# Week 6 Readings — Phase 1 Integration

## Overview
本周是 Phase 1 的整合復習週。我們將整合化學 → 生物化學 → 細胞生物學 → 生理學的完整知識體系，並通過臨床案例和模擬項目進行綜合應用。

## Required Readings

### Primary Integration Texts
- **Campbell Biology, 11th ed.** (Reece et al., 2017) — Chapters 1-10 (full review)
- **Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell, 6th ed.** (2014) — Chapters 1-4 (Introduction, Cell Chemistry, Proteins, DNA)
- **Guyton & Hall, Textbook of Medical Physiology, 14th ed.** (2020) — Chapters 1-10 (Basic physiology principles)

### Integration and Systems Biology
1. **Kitano, H.** (2002) "Systems Biology: A Brief Overview" — *Science*, 295(5560), 1662-1664.
2. **Heng, H.H. et al.** (2010) "The Cell Theory and the Central Dogma" — *BioEssays*, 32(7), 607-613.

### Clinical Integration Papers
1. **DeFronzo, R.A.** (2009) "From the Hexosamine Biosynthetic Pathway to Diabetic Complications" — *Diabetes*, 58(11), 2460-2470.
2. **Lowell, B.B. & Shulman, G.I.** (2005) "Mitochondrial Dysfunction and Type 2 Diabetes" — *Science*, 307(5708), 384-387.

## Review Topics

### Week 1-2: Chemistry & Biomolecules
- Atomic structure and chemical bonds
- Water properties and pH
- Carbon chemistry and functional groups
- Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids
- Enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten)

### Week 3: Bioenergetics
- ATP as energy currency
- Glycolysis and fermentation
- Citric acid cycle (Krebs cycle)
- Electron transport chain and oxidative phosphorylation
- Chemiosmotic theory (Peter Mitchell, Nobel 1978)

### Week 4: Cell Biology
- Fluid Mosaic Model (Singer & Nicolson, 1972)
- Membrane transport (passive, active, vesicular)
- Cytoskeleton and cell motility
- Cell signaling pathways (GPCR, RTK)
- Protein sorting and organelle function

### Week 5: Homeostasis & Physiology
- Negative feedback systems
- Action potential (Hodgkin & Huxley, Nobel 1963)
- Nervous system organization
- Endocrine system and hormones
- Blood glucose and temperature regulation

## Assessment
- **Phase 1 Mock Exam**: Comprehensive test covering Weeks 1-5
- **Integration Essay**: Connect concepts across all weeks
- **Portfolio Review**: Self-assessment of Phase 1 learning

## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 6, you should be able to:
1. Connect atomic structure to molecular function
2. Explain how enzyme kinetics relates to metabolic regulation
3. Trace energy flow from glucose to ATP
4. Describe how membrane structure enables transport and signaling
5. Integrate cellular and systemic physiology
6. Apply knowledge to clinical scenarios (diabetes, metabolic syndrome)
