## Learning Objectives (from HKU BMED2302)

By the end of Week 4, students should be able to:
1. Explain the Fluid Mosaic Model of membrane structure
2. Describe the major membrane organelles and their functions
3. Calculate diffusion rates using Fick's law
4. Distinguish between passive, facilitated, and active transport
5. Explain the sodium-potassium pump mechanism
6. Understand vesicular transport (exocytosis/endocytosis)
7. Connect cell biology to BME applications (biosensors, drug delivery)
