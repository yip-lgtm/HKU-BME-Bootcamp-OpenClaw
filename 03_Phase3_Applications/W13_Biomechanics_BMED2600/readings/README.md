## Learning Objectives (BMED2600)
By the end of Week 13, students should be able to:
1. Define stress (σ), strain (ε), and apply Hooke's Law σ = Eε to calculate material response
2. Distinguish between elastic, plastic, and viscoelastic deformation behavior
3. Derive and apply the Maxwell and Kelvin-Voigt viscoelastic models
4. Explain the quasi-linear viscoelastic (QLV) theory of Fung for soft tissues
5. Compare mechanical properties of cortical bone, trabecular bone, tendon, ligament, and cartilage

## Assessment Alignment
- BMED2600 Assignment 1 (Week 15): Stress-strain analysis of bone under tensile loading
- BMED2600 Mid-term (Week 16): Viscoelastic models, creep, stress relaxation
